Reflexivity of equality

Claim. Every natural number equals itself.

This is an instance of reflexivity: any object is equal to itself. For example, 3 = 3. The statement here concerns every natural number.

The separate annotation selects the claim above and associates it with the formal example. This article remains an independent document.
